One line about a quiet, earned moment of pride, written for the [make.fun bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/u-mang-one-line-of-earned-pride-a0138557).

The pride in this line is wordless: forty years of work handed over in a single gesture, no speech, no applause.

## Entry

> The old fisherman mended his nets alone for forty years; this morning his grandson sat beside him, threaded the needle right on the first try, and the old man said nothing. He just handed him the net.
